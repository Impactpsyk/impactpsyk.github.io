Privacy Policy for Impact Smart Pack

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Impact Smart Pack is a travel packing-list application developed by Impact Group. This Privacy Policy explains how Impact Smart Pack handles information when you use the app.

Information You Enter

Impact Smart Pack allows you to enter and manage information such as:

  • trip destinations and dates
  • packing lists and item quantities
  • traveler profiles and preferences
  • bag types and weight allowances
  • inventory of owned items
  • activity preferences
  • flight details and reminders
  • trip feedback and reviews
  • custom item names, colors, and notes

Impact Smart Pack does not require you to create an account.

Your packing data is stored locally on your device. Impact Group does not operate a cloud server that receives or stores your personal packing database.

Local Data Storage

Packing information created in Impact Smart Pack is stored locally on your device using a local database.

You can remove this information by deleting records within the app, using the Reset App Data function in Settings, clearing the app's data through Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Depending on your device settings, Android's own backup feature may include this local data in your own backup (for example, in your Google account). Those backups are controlled by you and your device provider; Impact Group cannot access them.

You are responsible for any backups or exports you create.

Weather and Destination Information

Impact Smart Pack retrieves weather forecast data from Open-Meteo, a free weather API, to provide weather-based packing recommendations.

When you create a trip, the app sends the destination name you entered to Open-Meteo to find its location, and then sends the destination's geographic coordinates together with your trip dates to retrieve forecast or historical weather data. The app does not send device identifiers or user accounts to Open-Meteo. However, as with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the service when the request is made.

Weather data is cached locally on your device.

Currency Exchange Rates

Impact Smart Pack retrieves currency exchange rate data from the Frankfurter API to provide currency information for trip destinations.

The app sends only currency codes (such as USD or EUR) to the Frankfurter API. The app does not send device identifiers or user accounts. However, as with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the service when the request is made.

Exchange rate data is cached locally on your device.

Import and Export

Impact Smart Pack includes import and export features that allow you to transfer packing data between devices or create backups.

Exported files may contain trip details, packing lists, profiles, and preferences. You control where exported files are saved or shared and are responsible for protecting exported files.

Data leaves the device only when you explicitly initiate an export, share, or transfer action.

Camera and Device-to-Device Transfer

Impact Smart Pack can transfer packing data between your own devices using a QR code. The sending device displays a QR code; the receiving device scans it with its camera.

The app asks for camera permission only when you choose to receive data from another device and scan a QR code. The camera image is processed on your device only to read the QR code. It is not saved, uploaded, or shared. The transfer does not use an Impact Group server.

Camera permission is optional. If you decline it, all other features remain available, including file-based export and import. Impact Smart Pack does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, files, or location.

Notifications

Impact Smart Pack may send local device notifications for flight reminders and packing prompts. Notifications are generated locally on the device and do not transmit data to external servers.

You may enable or disable notifications through device settings.

Advertising

Impact Smart Pack displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • app and user interactions
  • diagnostic information
  • device and account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers where available

Google may use this information for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Impact Smart Pack does not sell your personal packing records to advertisers.

For information about Google's data practices, please review Google's privacy policy and information relating to Google advertising.

Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Impact Smart Pack uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

Depending on your location and applicable law, Google may display a consent or privacy message before advertising requests are permitted.

Where required, Impact Smart Pack also provides access to Google's privacy-options interface through Settings → Privacy Choices, so that applicable advertising privacy choices can be reviewed or changed.

Voluntary Donations

Impact Smart Pack may include voluntary donation options through external payment links (such as PayPal or PromptPay). These are external services not integrated into the app.

Impact Smart Pack does not process, collect, or store payment information. Donation payment processing is handled entirely by the external payment provider.

Third-Party Services

Impact Smart Pack currently integrates with services including:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management)
  • Open-Meteo (weather forecasts)
  • Frankfurter API (currency exchange rates)

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Impact Group does not control data that these services independently collect as part of providing their functionality.

Data Sharing

Impact Group does not sell your personal packing information.

Your packing database is not uploaded to an Impact Group cloud server.

Information may nevertheless be transmitted to third-party services:

  • to Open-Meteo when retrieving weather forecasts — destination names, destination coordinates, trip dates, and your device's IP address;
  • to the Frankfurter API when retrieving currency exchange rates — currency codes and your device's IP address;
  • to Google as part of AdMob advertising and consent management — advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information as described in the Advertising section; or
  • when you explicitly initiate an export, share, or similar transfer action.

Data Security

Impact Smart Pack uses local device storage for your packing information.

Communications with external services (Open-Meteo, Frankfurter API, Google services) use HTTPS encryption.

Users are responsible for maintaining device security, controlling physical access, and protecting exported files.

Data Deletion

Impact Smart Pack does not require an account.

You can delete locally stored packing information by:

  • deleting individual trips, items, or records inside the app;
  • using the Reset App Data function in Settings;
  • clearing Impact Smart Pack's application data in Android settings; or
  • uninstalling Impact Smart Pack.

If you need assistance regarding data associated with Impact Smart Pack, contact us using the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Impact Smart Pack is intended for general audiences.

The app is not directed to children under 13.

User Rights

GDPR (EEA/UK/Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the GDPR including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, port, and object to processing of your personal data. Because Impact Smart Pack stores data locally on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly through the app. For formal requests, contact us at the email address below.

CCPA/CPRA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA and CPRA. Impact Group does not sell personal information. When you provide consent through the privacy message shown in the app, Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers and interaction data for personalized advertising purposes, which may constitute "sharing" under the CCPA. You can manage or withdraw this consent at any time through Settings → Privacy Choices. You may exercise other applicable rights by contacting us at the email address below.
